Mark Minervini's Stage 2 Trend Template

Derived from the Specific Entry Point Analysis (SEPA®) methodology pioneered by U.S. Investing Champion Mark Minervini, the Stage 2 Trend Template is designed to ensure you only purchase stocks in confirmed, institutional uptrends.

01

Why Stage 2 Only?

Over 95% of all monster stock moves occur during Stage 2 advancing phases. By strictly excluding Stage 1 accumulation bases, Stage 3 topping patterns, and Stage 4 declines, you avoid dead money and debilitating drawdown risk.

02

The Overhead Supply Law

Stocks trading more than 25% below their 52-week highs are trapped under thousands of underwater investors waiting to sell on every rally. Trading near 52-week highs eliminates bagholder resistance.

03

The Baseline Before VCP

A Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP) cannot form in a vacuum. The Trend Template establishes the mandatory structural foundation before progressive volatility contraction can resolve into explosive breakouts.
